RESUME RANKING SYSTEM DOCUMENTATION
Complete Implementation Guide

🎯 WHAT IS THIS?
A recruiter-lens evaluation system that scores resumes exactly like a professional recruiter would, providing:

Overall score (0-100)
Interview probability (realistic %)
6 category breakdowns (detailed scoring)
Actionable improvements (specific next steps)
Honest assessment (strengths, weaknesses, red flags)

This transforms your platform from "resume generator" to "career coach."

📊 THE 6 EVALUATION CATEGORIES
1. ATS Optimization (20% weight)
What It Measures:

Keyword match to job description
ATS-friendly formatting
Standard section headers
Proper bullet points (not unicode)

Scoring:

90-100: Will rank high in ATS searches
75-89: Good keyword presence
60-74: Missing some keywords
40-59: Will be filtered out
0-39: Auto-rejected

Common Issues:

Missing critical keywords (Java, React, etc.)
Using tables or columns (ATS can't parse)
Non-standard section headers
Unicode bullets (•) instead of standard


2. Human Readability (20% weight)
What It Measures:

6-second scan test (can recruiter understand quickly?)
Bullet length (2 lines max)
Metric visibility
Visual hierarchy
Information density

Scoring:

90-100: Pleasure to read, metrics jump out
75-89: Clean and scannable
60-74: Takes effort to find key info
40-59: Difficult to scan
0-39: Overwhelming, will be skipped

Common Issues:

Bullets wrapping to 3+ lines
Walls of text in paragraphs
Metrics buried in sentences
Poor visual hierarchy


3. Job Match (25% weight - HIGHEST)
What It Measures:

Technology match percentage
Industry experience relevance
Seniority level appropriateness
Critical gaps
Transferable skills

Scoring:

90-100: Perfect match, exceeds requirements
75-89: Meets all key requirements
60-74: Some gaps but transferable
40-59: Significant gaps
0-39: Wrong background

Common Issues:

Missing required technologies
Wrong seniority level
No industry experience
Critical skill gaps


4. Impact Communication (20% weight)
What It Measures:

Metric density (% of bullets with numbers)
Achievement vs. responsibility
Metric quality (impressive and specific?)
Business impact shown
Realistic and verifiable

Scoring:

90-100: Every bullet has metrics, impressive achievements
75-89: Most bullets have metrics
60-74: Some bullets have metrics
40-59: Few metrics, mostly responsibilities
0-39: No metrics, generic descriptions

Common Issues:

Generic bullets without numbers
"Responsible for..." instead of accomplishments
Vague statements ("improved performance")
No business impact beyond technical work


5. Professional Presentation (10% weight)
What It Measures:

Polish and professionalism
Formatting consistency
Appropriate length
Grammar and spelling
Tone appropriateness

Scoring:

90-100: Flawless, executive-level
75-89: Professional, minor issues
60-74: Acceptable but rough
40-59: Multiple issues
0-39: Unprofessional

Common Issues:

Typos and grammar errors
Inconsistent formatting
Too long (3+ pages)
Casual tone for formal role


6. Truthfulness (5% weight but can override)
What It Measures:

Claims seem realistic?
Metrics believable?
No contradictions?
Employment gaps explained?
Red flags present?

Scoring:

90-100: Completely credible
75-89: Mostly credible
60-74: Some concerns
40-59: Multiple red flags
0-39: Obvious fabrication

RED FLAGS (Auto-reject if detected):

Fabricated job titles or companies
Impossible metrics ("improved performance 500%")
Technologies claimed but never used
Timeline inconsistencies
Suspicious job hopping (5 jobs in 2 years)
